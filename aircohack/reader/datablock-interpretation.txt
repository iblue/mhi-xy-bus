If AC sends byte 7 = 0x48, the temperature value of something has changed and
in byte 14, this temperature is sent.
- It is stable when the unit is turned on, with values around 0xc5 - 0xc9 (34.5 °C - 36.5 °C)
- When the unit is turned of, it increases up to 0xcb (37.5 °C), then sharply dropping
- The lowest value is 0x9d (14.5 °C)
- It increases in the early morning to max 36.5 °C, then drops again.

In the same packet (byte 7 = 0x48), byte 10 is ist to 0x02, then a few minutes
after the unit turned of, the value goes to 0x00 and the temperature (see
above) starts to drop.

It may be some thermistor from the outside unit, because in the morning in
increases, if the sun is shining on the outside unit.
