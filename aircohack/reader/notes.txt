# Byte 1: Master Address of target (00..0f) (80 = answer)
# Byte 2: Slave unit address (07 .. 77) of target (01 = answer)
# Byte 3: On/off, mode
  - a2: Auto, aus
  - aa: aus                1 010 101 0
  - ab: an, kuehlen        1 010 101 1
  - af: an, lueften        1 010 111 1
  - f3: an, heizen         1 111 001 1
  - a3: an, auto           1 010 001 1
  - a7: an, entfeuchten    1 010 011 1
# Byte 4: Fan
  - 98: Level 1              10011000
  - 99: Level 2              10011001
  - 9a: Level 3              10011010
  - aa: heizen (fan war aus) 10101010
# Byte 5: Target temperature (X-0x80)/2.0 = Temp in °C
  - a4: 18.0 (Scale incorrect?)
  - a5: 18.5
  - a6: 19.0
  - a7: 19.5
  - a8: 20.0
  - a9: 20.5
  - aa: 21.0
  - ab: 21.5
  - ac: 22.0
  - ad: 22.5
  - ae: 23.0 (DEFAULT)
# Byte 6: Outside temperature, temp at unit, compressor temp?
  - 0xff for RC
  - other values for AC

# Byte 7-15: 9 bytes of additional data block???
- Currently unknown. Used for maintenance.
- RC just sends 00 00 80 00 00 ff ff ff ff if there is nothing to do (after power loss)
-               00 00 90 00 00 ff ff ff ff otherwise
- AC answers with 00 00 88 00 ff ff ff ff ff (power loss)
-                 00 00 c8 00 ff ff ff ff ff otherwise

# Byte 9:
- After power loss
  - From RC: 0x80
  - From AC: 0x88
- After system turned on, ca. 10-20 seconds afterwards
  - From RC: 0x90
  - From AC: 0xc8
- After system turned off again, after some time:
  - From RC: 0x90
  - From AC: 0x48

AC can also send 0x08, then whole block becomes:
  08 00 ff 35 10 0a 01
AC can also send 0x48, then:
  48 00 ff 80 10 b5 ff
  48 00 ff 80 10 b6 ff
